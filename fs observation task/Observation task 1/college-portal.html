Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) is an autonomous engineering institution located in Sangivalasa, near Visakhapatnam, established in 2001. Affiliated with Andhra University and accredited with a NAAC "A+" grade, the college offers a range of B.Tech programs, including Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, and Data Science. Spread across a 12-acre campus, ANITS combines strong academic infrastructure with active placement support, giving students a solid foundation to build technical skills and launch careers in the tech industry.

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS), Visakhapatnam is owned by Megha Engineering and Infrastructure Limited (MEIL) which is one of the top infrastructure and manufacturing company in India with headquarters at Hyderabad. The institute was established in the academic year 2001-02 by Anil Neerukonda Educational Society (ANES) which was founded by Dr. N.B.R. Prasad, an NRI Philanthropist from the USA, with industrialists and eminent educationists in memory of his son Late Anil Neerukonda.
